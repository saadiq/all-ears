The clay under the back border stays wet all winter, so I forked in leaf mould and coarse grit last autumn.

Slugs shredded the young hostas again, but the copper ring around the raised bed seems to be holding them off.

I moved the compost heap nearer the shed and turned it twice, and the middle is already warm enough to steam.

Pruning the espaliered pear too late cost me most of the fruiting spurs, so this year I cut back in midsummer.

The greenhouse vents jam whenever the humidity climbs, so I prop them open with a length of bamboo cane.
